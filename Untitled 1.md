## AI EVALUATION-RELEVANT SKILLS

I am particularly interested in evaluating AI systems from a technical and cybersecurity perspective.

My approach to reviewing an AI response includes checking:

**Accuracy**  
Is the technical information factually correct?

**Reasoning**  
Does the conclusion logically follow from the information provided?

**Consistency**  
Does the response contradict itself or contain internal inconsistencies?

**Relevance**  
Does the response actually answer the question that was asked?

**Instruction Following**  
Did the response follow the requirements and constraints of the task?

**Security & Safety**  
Could the response introduce a security vulnerability, unsafe recommendation, or other significant risk?

**Clarity**  
Is the explanation understandable and technically precise?

**Evidence & Verification**  
Where appropriate, can important technical claims be checked against reliable information?
